## Improved

- Dragged icons now make room as soon as they pass a neighbour, with no pause.
- Holding an app over a folder now grows the folder smoothly.
- Dragging an app to the edge of a folder's last page now adds a new page.

## Fixed

- Clicking outside a folder or pressing Escape while it opens now closes it.
- Dragging an app out of a folder no longer removes the folder when one app is left.
- Deleting an app no longer leaves a gap while the app is being removed.
