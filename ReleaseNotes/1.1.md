## New

- The Dock's Apps icon now shows Launchpad's icon; turn this off with **Use the Launchpad Icon for Apps**.
- Icons in the Dock can be dragged and removed while Launchpad is open.

## Improved

- The app icons are simpler, and LaunchpadAwakener's own icon carries a gear to tell it apart from Launchpad.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where input methods stopped working in Launchpad's search field and folder names.
- Fixed an issue where Launchpad froze after asking whether to delete an app.
- In **Organize Launchpad**, dropping onto a folder in the sidebar no longer puts apps on its page instead.
- **Organize Launchpad** no longer flickers after a drop.
