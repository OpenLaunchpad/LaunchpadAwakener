The first release: macOS 15's Launchpad on macOS 26 and macOS 27, on Macs with Apple silicon.

## New

- LaunchpadAwakener puts Launchpad back into the Dock, opened by the Launchpad key, the Dock's Apps icon or a hot corner.
- Launchpad, a standalone app, shows the same Launchpad in its own window without changing System Integrity Protection.
- Pages, folders and their order are imported from the layout macOS 15 left on your Mac.
- Search, folders, drag to arrange, deleting App Store apps and the blue dot on new apps work as in macOS 15.
- Input methods work in the search field and when renaming a folder.
- Drag an app out of Launchpad onto the Dock to keep it there.
- **Organize Launchpad** moves many apps at once, sorts them by name and groups them into folders by App Store category.

## Known Issues

- LaunchpadAwakener needs System Integrity Protection fully disabled; with it on, it offers to install the standalone app instead.
- The pinch gesture is not confirmed to work.
